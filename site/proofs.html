Habitat · portable evidence

Proof snapshots.

Historical Habitat proof exports copied into AgentScope. Each bundle is checked in your browser for structure, its SHA-256 digest, a separately pinned manifest digest and claim/action consistency.

Important: a matching content hash detects changes to the exported bundle, but does not authenticate who published it. These are historical records, not live status. An unsigned proof does not independently establish that the claimed work happened in the outside world. The ledger integrity value is the result recorded by Habitat at export time; this page does not recompute the underlying SQLite action hash chain.
