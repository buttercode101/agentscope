A small public index of historical Habitat proof exports copied into the AgentScope static site. Each bundle is checked in your browser for structural consistency and its embedded SHA-256 content digest.

Read this carefully: a matching content hash detects accidental or deliberate changes to the exported bundle, but does not authenticate who published it. These snapshots are historical records, not live status. An unsigned proof does not independently establish that the claimed work happened in the outside world.
